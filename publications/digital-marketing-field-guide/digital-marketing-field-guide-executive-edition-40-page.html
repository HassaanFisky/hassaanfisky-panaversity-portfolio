CHAPTER 01 / CUSTOMER TRUTHFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Customer Truth & Irresistible Offer Design

Ad se pehle customer ko samjho. Warna budget message ko nahi, confusion ko amplify karega.

Customer buys progress, not a product feature

A person hires a product or service to make progress in a real situation: save time, reduce risk, complete a task, feel confident or get a better result.

Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

JTBD is the progress a customer wants to make in a specific context. It is not simply a demographic or an interest. Ask what happened, what they tried, what they want to change and why now.

CHAPTER 01 / OFFER DESIGNFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Make the Offer Easy to Choose

A good offer says who it helps, what problem it solves, what is included and what happens next.

Write to Customer + Write to Problem + Write Clear Offer

Write to a specific buyer. Name the problem they recognise. State the offer in concrete words with price, terms, proof and one action.

Value proposition formula

We help [specific customer] solve [specific problem] with [clear benefit], without [main friction]. Replace every bracket with a real fact, not a vague promise.

Offer anatomy

PartWhat must be clear
Product / serviceExactly what is delivered
Price + termsTotal payable amount, duration and exclusions
ProofReal demo, verified review, credential or measurable detail
Risk reducerOnly a return, warranty, trial or support promise the business honours
UrgencyA real stock or deadline limit, never a fake countdown
One CTAOne clear link, reply, booking or purchase action

Before / after

BeforeAfter
“Best quality at the best price.”“A compact desk lamp for students who need focused light without taking over a small study table.”
“Marketing services for everyone.”“A weekly campaign review for small teams that need to find wasted spend and clarify the next action.”
Offer checkpoint

Clear customer? Clear problem? Full price? Proof available? Risk reducer real? Urgency honest? Next action obvious?

Attention Psychology & Feed Algorithms

Attention is earned by relevance and viewer satisfaction, not by tricks.

Feed ranking, in plain English

A feed ranking system decides the order a viewer sees eligible content. A recommender system selects or suggests items using predictions about what may be useful or interesting to that viewer.

How ranking works

Eligible content

↓
Predicted value
↓

↓
Future signals

Signals to read together

SignalWhat it can indicate
Watch timeHow long a viewer stayed with the content
CompletionWhether the viewer reached the end
ReplayWhether the viewer watched again
SavesThe viewer wants to return to the content
SharesThe viewer chose to send it to someone
Meaningful commentsA real question, opinion or conversation
Hides / reportsNegative feedback or lack of relevance
No single magic signal

Signals vary by platform, format, viewer and context. No universal ranking formula or fixed public weighting should be assumed.

CHAPTER 02 / FIRST FRAMEFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Win the First Two Seconds

Make the right person understand why they should stay. Shock is not a strategy.

First frame: viewer + problem + reason

Name the viewer or situation, show the friction and give one honest reason to keep watching. The on-screen frame and spoken hook should support the same idea.

Four hook types

TypeExample structure
Problem“Still losing time because [specific friction]?”
Outcome“Here is one way to [specific result].”
Contrast“Before: [friction]. After: [real visible change].”
Specific question“Does [specific situation] happen in your workflow?”

Sound-off design map

ElementCheck
One visual ideaOne frame, one clear subject
Readable copyText can be read on a phone without zoom
CaptionsSpoken meaning is visible
Strong contrastText and background remain distinct
ProofShow an action, not just a claim
CTAOne next step, not several
Weak → clear

Weak: “Our product is amazing.” Clear: “A desk organiser for small study tables. See what fits before choosing.”

Mobile QA

Test at phone size, with sound off and after platform crop. Keep key content in safe zones.

Meta Business Portfolio & Account Security

Protect the asset first. Grant people only the access their work requires.

Ownership and access map

↓

Two things are not the same

An individual person signs in with their own real account. A Business Portfolio organizes business assets and permissions. It is not a shared login.

Permission principle

SituationSafer approach
Internal team memberAdd the person, then assign only the assets needed for the task.
Agency / other businessUse the partner workflow and the partner business ID requested by Meta.
Temporary contractorGive minimum access, set an owner and remove access when the work ends.
Former staffReview and revoke their asset access and active sessions.
Never share passwords

Use individual identity, two-factor authentication (2FA) and controlled asset permissions. Password sharing removes accountability.

CHAPTER 05 / INCIDENT RESPONSEFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Account Quality & Security Review

A restriction is a signal to find the cause, not a reason to evade the rules.

Safe incident response

Read the exact notice
↓
Classify: policy / payment / identity / security
↓
Fix the underlying issue
↓
Submit accurate documents if requested
↓
Request review via official support
↓
Record the outcome and audit access

Never use these workarounds

  • Fake documents or borrowed identities
  • Cloaking or misleading landing pages
  • Repeated account creation to bypass a restriction
  • Sharing a password or recovery code

Monthly owner checklist

Confirm 2FA and recovery. Review active people and partners. Remove access that is no longer needed. Check billing and invoices. Review account notices. Keep a named internal owner for each asset.

Security worksheet

Example trigger: If 2FA is Off → open Business Settings → Security Centre → Two-factor authentication → require 2FA before granting partner access. If an unfamiliar admin appears → open People → select that person → Remove access → review recent activity and billing changes. Assign the business owner, ad-account owner and billing owner by role before the next campaign.

CHAPTER 06 / TESTINGFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Audience & Creative: Clean Experiments

Do not change five things and then guess which one caused the result.

Audience options

ApproachMeaningUse carefully
BroadAvoid unnecessary narrowing within real business limitsNeeds clear offer and useful creative
InterestA category / interest hypothesisInterest is not proof of buying intent
CustomEligible first-party audience dataRespect consent and privacy
Lookalike / similarAudience built from a source group where offeredSource quality matters

Change one primary variable

For example: keep the offer, audience, budget, destination and test window stable; compare two hooks. A result is easier to interpret when one main factor changes.

Creative fatigue signals

Look at frequency with response trends, rising cost, negative feedback, delivery conditions and actual sales. Frequency by itself is not a stop rule.

Test record

Example test: If Hook A records 2.1% CTR and Hook B 1.4% at the same audience and spend → open Ads Reporting → compare cost per qualified purchase and contribution → keep the stronger delivered-order result, not the higher click rate alone. Sample guardrail: cap the test at PKR 3,000; pause and inspect event tracking if no qualified purchase appears.

COD vs Prepayment: Follow the Money

The right result is cash collected after fulfilment costs, not the order count on the dashboard.

The denominator changes the decision

New-customer CAC = PKR 45,000 ÷ 70 delivered first-time buyers = PKR 642.86. Contribution after ads per delivered buyer = PKR 49,000 ÷ 70 = PKR 700.

COD vs prepayment: choose by the buyer and the cost

COD can lower payment hesitation but creates refusal, courier and settlement exposure. Prepayment improves collection timing but requires trusted payment rails, clear refund terms and proof.

Compare delivered-and-paid contribution by payment method. Do not call orders placed, payment captured, or courier status alone a profit result.

Do not misuse JIT

True Just-in-Time is a replenishment approach that aligns stock arrival with demand. It is not automatically the same as dropshipping or ordering from an unverified supplier after each sale.

First question

Who is responsible when stock is wrong, late, damaged, returned or unavailable? Write the answer before you sell.

CHAPTER 08 / INVENTORY CONTROLFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Stock, Control & the Variant Mix

Inventory-light does not mean risk-free. The seller must know who holds the item and who can actually control it.

Possession and control are different

Possession means who physically holds the stock. Control means who can verify availability, reserve it, approve dispatch and act when something changes.

ModelPossessionControl to verify
Direct store with own stockUsually the business / its warehouse partnerLive count, quality checks, dispatch priority

Inventory-light resellerSupplierLive stock, price, quality, dispatch and replacement may be weak
AggregatorSeller / providerPlatform rules, records, service levels and dispute process

Move slow stock without misleading the buyer

Protect the best-selling variant, then test a relevant bundle that gives a slow-moving variant a genuine reason to be chosen.

  • Keep the hero product available; do not let a promotion empty the only proven seller.
  • Pair a slower variant only when the use-case fits and the price is clear.
  • Show the actual variant, dimensions, condition and bundle contents.
  • Compare contribution and return rate by SKU, not revenue alone.
Supplier-control gate

If stock, quality, dispatch time or replacement terms cannot be confirmed → do not promise them in the ad or chat → secure written confirmation or choose another supply route.

Aggregator vs hyperlocal

Aggregator describes the relationship: a platform connects buyers with sellers or providers. Hyperlocal describes geography: service happens within a nearby zone. One business can be both.

Disintermediation / bypass

After meeting on the platform, buyer and seller may move the deal to a direct phone call or WhatsApp. The platform can lose its fee, records and ability to support the transaction.

Retention comes from value

Verified profiles, useful discovery, clear pricing, reliable booking, payment trust, tracking, dispute support, loyalty and seller tools. No policy guarantees that bypass becomes impossible.

Define the basis

CHAPTER 10 / EXPERIMENTSFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Scientific Testing & Scaling Decisions

A useful test is a decision with a hypothesis, a boundary and a way to judge the result.

Write a testable hypothesis

Example: “A problem-led opening will produce more qualified visits than a product-only opening for this audience.” It states one change and a measurable outcome.

Test card

FieldWrite before launch
ControlWhat stays unchanged?
VariableWhat one main factor changes?
Primary metricWhich result decides the test?
GuardrailWhat must not get worse?
WindowWhen will you review?
Loss limitHow much can you afford to risk?

Incrementality

Incrementality asks whether marketing caused extra outcomes that would not have happened otherwise. Attribution assigns credit; incrementality tests added impact.

Cohort and payback

A cohort groups customers by a shared trait such as first purchase period. Retention measures whether they return. Payback is how long it takes to recover acquisition cost from contribution.

Avoid false certainty

Seasonality, small samples, offer changes and uneven traffic can mislead. Record uncertainty; do not scale on one lucky day.

CHAPTER 11 / AI CO-PILOTFIELD GUIDE / OFFICIAL EDITION
Copy-Paste Prompts for Practical Campaign Work

AI can speed up structured thinking. It cannot verify facts that were never supplied.

AUTO-PERSONALIZING CAMPAIGN STRATEGIST
You are my practical campaign strategist. Ask exactly these 3 questions, one at a time: (1) What is the product/service and selling price? (2) Who is the target buyer and what city/country do you serve? (3) What is the daily budget and sales channel: website, store, WhatsApp, marketplace or calls? After I answer, summarize my details and ask me to approve saving them to memory/profile before retaining anything. Never claim persistent memory if you do not have it. Then create audience hypotheses, 2-second hooks, ad copy, creative angles and short WhatsApp closing scripts. Ask for proof and policy limits before writing claims. Label assumptions and examples. Never invent reviews, results, prices or guarantees.
ZERO-FLUFF PROFIT & AD AUDITOR
First ask me for exactly 4 numbers: selling price, product/delivery cost, ad spend and confirmed orders. Tell me clearly that these four inputs cannot produce exact net contribution when return/RTO, COD/payment fees, tax, packaging, discount, failed delivery and refund costs are missing. Calculate only supported provisional values and show each formula. Then request delivered and paid orders, new customers, returns/RTO and missing variable costs before making a final decision. CAC uses new paying customers, not leads. Give FIX / HOLD / STOP / SCALE with assumptions and no false precision.
