# Companion Video — Digital Marketing: From Attention to Actual Profit

**Format:** 16:9 master, with 9:16 cut-downs for Shorts/Reels  
**Target runtime:** 2:40–3:00  
**Voice:** calm, practical, specific. No hype.  
**Visual system:** warm ivory, deep navy, restrained teal and muted gold. Use the book’s own diagrams and interface mockups; do not imply they are live platform screenshots.

## Production notes

1. Record the voiceover in a quiet room; use a natural speaking pace and leave a half-second after each key formula.
2. Keep all examples visibly labelled **Illustrative example**. Do not present sample numbers as market benchmarks.
3. Use the actual book diagrams as source visuals. Avoid fake screenshots of a live Meta account.
4. For the 9:16 cut, keep the calculator, main labels and CTA inside the central safe area; recompose rather than cropping 16:9 text.
5. Export a captioned master and a sound-off version. Check the captions at phone size.
6. Final end card: the book cover, author name and the verified social links supplied in the publication.
