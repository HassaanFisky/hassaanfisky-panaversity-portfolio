**[00:00–00:08 | Hook]**  
Clicks aa rahe hain. Messages bhi aa rahe hain. Lekin agar delivered orders ke baad paisa nahi bach raha, to campaign successful nahi hai.

**[00:08–00:23 | Reframe]**  
Digital marketing sirf ad publish karna nahi. Yeh aik connected business system hai: customer ki zaroorat, clear offer, relevant message, smooth buying journey, aur sahi financial measurement.

**[00:23–00:41 | Customer and offer]**  
Pehle yeh samjhein ke customer asal mein kis progress ke liye pay kar raha hai. Uska trigger kya hai? Woh kis alternative ko use kar raha hai? Kis objection ki wajah se rukta hai? Phir offer ko seedha likhein: customer, problem, clear offer.

**[00:41–00:59 | Attention and creative]**  
Creative ka pehla kaam shock dena nahi. Sahi person ko pehle seconds mein yeh samjhana hai ke video uske liye kyun relevant hai. Sound off par bhi message clear rakhein. Hook ke baad demo ya real proof dikhayein, phir aik simple next step.

**[00:59–01:19 | Campaign and conversation]**  
Paid campaign mein teen levels yaad rakhein: Campaign, Ad Set, Ad. Campaign outcome set karta hai. Ad Set audience, budget, schedule aur placements handle karta hai. Ad actual message aur destination dikhata hai. Aur WhatsApp enquiry ko sale tab count karein jab order confirm, deliver aur pay ho.

**[01:19–01:43 | Financial reality]**  
ROAS profit nahi hota. Pehle delivered order ka contribution nikaalein: customer se net revenue minus product, packaging, payment, delivery, COD aur return-related variable costs. Phir acquisition cost minus karein. CAC ko new paying customers se divide karein, leads se nahi.

**[01:43–02:02 | Decision]**  
Agar tracking, offer ya checkout toot raha hai, Fix. Data kam hai, Hold. Loss limit cross ho gayi, Stop. Contribution positive hai, result repeatable hai, aur fulfilment ready hai, tab Scale.

**[02:02–02:15 | Close]**  
Growth ka matlab sirf zyada spend nahi. Growth ka matlab hai behtar decisions, cleaner numbers aur customer ke liye behtar experience. Yehi is Digital Marketing Field Guide ka core hai.

- Capture real screen footage only after hiding account IDs, customer data, spend details and other private information.
- Add accurate English/Roman Urdu captions; review all names, numbers and formulas.
- Keep key text in the central mobile-safe area; check at phone size.
- Use music, fonts, icons and footage with a suitable licence or written permission.
- Export a clean master with captions and a separate clean version without burned-in captions.

## LinkedIn launch copy — approval draft

Clicks are easy to celebrate. Profitable orders are harder to earn.

I’ve been building a practical Digital Marketing Field Guide around one operating idea:

**Marketing works when customer truth, the offer, the creative, the buying journey and unit economics agree.**

The guide brings the work into one sequence:

- Understand what the customer is actually trying to solve.
- Make the offer clear enough to trust.
- Earn attention without misleading people.
- Build a clean path from ad to conversation to delivered order.
- Measure contribution, not just platform ROAS.
- Fix, hold, stop or scale from evidence.

This is not a promise of cheap leads or guaranteed sales. It is a practical way to make marketing decisions easier to explain — and easier to measure.

I’ll share the frameworks chapter by chapter.

## Editorial guardrails

1. No made-up metrics, testimonials, screenshots, case studies or guaranteed outcomes.
2. Separate platform-attributed revenue from paid, delivered order records.
3. Separate confirmed COD orders from delivered and paid orders.
4. Do not call a four-number profit estimate “exact” when return, payment, tax and failed-delivery costs are missing.
5. Confirm the final PDF and Canva publication before adding a public download link to any launch copy.
6. A public Git commit provides a dated, tamper-evident repository record; it does not alone prove legal authorship or replace registration/advice where required.
